About

I studied measurement and control engineering at ECUST, then moved into AI with an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Nottingham (Merit). My dissertation evaluated anomaly-detection methods on 2.9 million one-minute observations across 30 US equities, and concluded they're better supported as a risk-monitoring aid than as a profit signal.

What I enjoy most is the step from “this is confusing” to “here's a tool that makes it obvious”. Several of the demos above started as questions I had while learning photography, Git and LaTeX.

How I build

  1. Specify. Write down the goal, constraints and what “done” looks like.
  2. Generate. Pair with AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) to draft quickly.
  3. Verify. Unit tests, manual checks in the real page, and reading every diff.
  4. Iterate. Ship a small version, then refine from real use.
