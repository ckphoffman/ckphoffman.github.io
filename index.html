Spotting market stress without crying wolf识别市场压力，又不“狼来了”

MSc dissertation, risk analytics硕士论文，风险分析

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • TSB-AD
  • Time series
Situation背景
Automated trading needs an early warning when markets behave abnormally, but real market data has no “correct answer” labels to test against.
自动化交易需要在市场异常时提前预警，但真实市场数据没有“标准答案”可以用来检验模型。
What I did我做了什么
Benchmarked 23 anomaly-detection set-ups on 2,910,814 one-minute bars from 30 US equities in 2025, comparing price-only inputs with a richer view (OHLC, volume, trade count, VWAP). Built my own execution layer around the benchmark, and an evaluation framework that works without labels: proxy labels, top-1% precision, consensus events and forward risk-enrichment.
在 2025 年 30 只美股、共 2,910,814 根一分钟 K 线上，对 23 种异常检测配置做基准测试，比较“仅收盘价”与“多维数据（OHLC、成交量、成交笔数、VWAP）”两种输入。自建执行层适配基准框架，并设计了一套无需标签的评估方法：代理标签、前 1% 精度、共识事件和前瞻风险富集比。
Result结果
690 aligned runs, zero failures. LSTMAD was strongest; richer inputs helped neural models track volume-driven activity. High-score periods lined up with higher stress and larger moves over the next 30 minutes.
690 组对齐结果，零失败。LSTMAD 表现最好；多维输入让神经网络模型更好地捕捉成交量驱动的异动。高分时段与当期压力上升、以及之后 30 分钟的更大波动高度吻合。
Why it matters for CB与企业银行的关系
FX and rates risk management and payment monitoring face the same problem: flag the unusual early, without drowning people in false alarms, and be honest about what a model can and can’t tell you.
外汇与利率风险管理、支付监控面对的是同一个问题：尽早发现异常，又不让误报淹没团队，并且诚实地说明模型能说明什么、不能说明什么。

A system where every change leaves a trace每一次修改都留痕的系统

Coursework, controlled operations课程项目，受控业务系统

  • PHP
  • SQL
  • MariaDB
  • Docker
Situation背景
The brief: a traffic-police team needs one place to link people, vehicles, incidents and fines, with clear separation between what officers and administrators may do.
课程需求：交警团队需要一个统一平台，把人员、车辆、事故和罚款关联起来，并清晰区分警员和管理员的权限。
What I did我做了什么
Modelled nine linked entities in a relational database; built role-based workflows for reporting, search and fines; added SQL triggers and application logging so every create, edit and read is recorded and filterable by user, action, table and date.
在关系型数据库中建模九类关联实体；实现基于角色的上报、检索和罚款流程；通过 SQL 触发器和应用层日志记录每一次新增、修改和读取，并可按用户、操作、数据表和日期筛选。
Result结果
A containerised application delivered with setup scripts, a user manual and a technical manual, so someone else could run, use and maintain it.
交付一套容器化应用，附带部署脚本、用户手册和技术手册，让别人能够部署、使用和维护。
Why it matters for CB与企业银行的关系
Cash management and trust & agency services run on the same building blocks: entitlements, linked records and a complete audit trail. I understand why controls exist, not just how to follow them.
现金管理、信托与代理服务的底层正是这些模块：权限、关联记录和完整的审计轨迹。我理解“为什么要有控制”，而不只是“怎样遵守控制”。

Independent product, iteration独立产品，迭代

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Node

Situation背景
I wanted to learn English vocabulary from the videos I actually watch, with every word shown in its real context.
我想从自己真正在看的视频里学英语单词，并看到每个词在真实语境里的用法。
What I did我做了什么
v1 was a single extension with local storage and a hand-written lemmatiser. When it outgrew that, I re-architected it into an extension that captures subtitles, an API that stores and counts words, and a web app that jumps the video to any sentence.
v1 是一个带本地存储和手写词形还原的单一扩展。用户需求超出它的能力后，我把它重构为三部分：抓取字幕的扩展、存储与统计词频的 API，以及能一键跳转到任意例句的网页应用。
Result结果
A working full-stack product with concordance views, favourites, filters, and frequency counts that don’t inflate on re-watching.
一个可用的全栈产品：例句索引、收藏、筛选，以及不会因重复观看而虚高的词频统计。
Why it matters for CB与企业银行的关系
Product management in cash management is the same loop: understand the client’s workflow, launch something useful, and know when to rebuild rather than patch.
现金管理的产品管理也是同样的循环：理解客户的工作流程，先推出有用的东西，并判断何时该重构而不是打补丁。
